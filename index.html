Welcome!

My name is Katie, and this is my ongoing collection of free art resources.

I created this site so that I would have a way to organize and share the various resources that I've gathered and use for my own art. Every resource in the collection is free to use and can be found on the web using the links on this site. Some of the sites require you to make an account to use their full functionality, however, I have made sure to clearly mark those in their descriptions. The content on this site is aimed to help artists find accessible and safe sites to add to their toolbelts!

You can find and sort through the entirety of my collection under the Resources tab. If you have any questions, concerns, or websites/files that you think would be a good addition to the collection, then please head on over to the Contact page to send me a message.

Thank you for visiting my site, and happy arting!
